# POWER-BI-DASHBOARDS
This project portfolio features three Power BI dashboards: an HR analytics tool tracking employee attrition rates, a retail sales store performance tracker, and a competitive comparison of quick-commerce giants Blinkit and Zepto. 
Project Overview 

• Tools used: Microsoft Power BI, DAX, Power Query 
• Goal: Turn raw business data into clear visual insights for HR, retail, and e-commerce domains. 

HR Dashboard (Attrition Rate) 

• Tracks overall employee attrition and retention numbers. 
• Highlights trends by department, job role, and employee tenure. 
• Helps human resources spot high-risk groups and improve staff retention. 

Sales Dashboard 

• Monitors total sales revenue, profit margins, and units sold. 
• Breaks down performance by store location, product category, and time period. 
• Highlights top-selling products and periods of low sales growth. 

E-Commerce Comparison (Blinkit vs. Zepto) 

• Compares quick-commerce metrics between Blinkit and Zepto. 
• Analyzes delivery times, product pricing, and category availability. 
• Evaluates market presence and customer choice patterns for both platforms.
